# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery

## Project Overview

The Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery project aims to identify, evaluate, and address risks to business operations from disruptions. This plan helps ensure that critical functions can continue or recover quickly after an incident, contributing to the overall resilience of the organization.

## Objectives

- Identify risks that could disrupt business operations.
- Assess the impact and likelihood of disruption scenarios.
- Develop and implement continuity and recovery strategies.
- Monitor and review continuity plans and test results regularly.

## Deliverables

- **Business Impact Analysis Report**: Document detailing critical functions, dependencies, and recovery objectives.
- **Business Continuity Plan**: Procedures for sustaining operations during a disruption.
- **Disaster Recovery Plan**: Procedures for restoring systems, data, and services.
- **Continuity Management Plan**: Comprehensive plan outlining the process for managing continuity and recovery.

## Project Phases

### 1. Business Impact Analysis

- **Objective**: Identify critical functions and their recovery needs.
- **Activities**:
  - Inventory business processes and dependencies.
  - Determine recovery time and recovery point objectives.
  - Assess the impact of disruption scenarios.

### 2. Risk Assessment

- **Objective**: Evaluate threats and vulnerabilities to continuity.
- **Activities**:
  - Perform risk assessments on facilities, systems, and suppliers.
  - Assess the likelihood and impact of disruption events.
  - Prioritize risks based on severity and business impact.

### 3. Continuity and Recovery Planning

- **Objective**: Develop strategies to sustain and restore operations.
- **Activities**:
  - Define continuity and recovery strategies and procedures.
  - Assign roles and responsibilities for incident response.
  - Establish alternate sites, backups, and communication plans.

### 4. Implementation

- **Objective**: Implement continuity and recovery measures.
- **Activities**:
  - Execute continuity plans and recovery procedures.
  - Train teams and validate backup and recovery capabilities.
  - Adjust strategies as needed based on test results.

### 5. Testing, Monitoring and Review

- **Objective**: Continuously test, monitor, and review continuity plans.
- **Activities**:
  - Conduct tabletop exercises and recovery tests.
  - Regularly update plans, contact lists, and recovery objectives.
  - Communicate continuity status and updates to stakeholders.
